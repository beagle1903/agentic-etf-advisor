# Issue123 manifest sequencing amendment runbook

This is a non-executable governance procedure. [ADR 0045](../architecture/decisions/0045-issue123-manifest-sequencing-amendment.md) defines the exact one-path target and native event. Issue134 approval G authorizes this mechanism only. Held R is a distinct prospective reset grant; neither is the operational transition approval.

1. In the original Issue123 repository and ledger, confirm the immutable 49-event prefix, full current history, exact generation-6 capsule, original approved owner, old amendment markers, and every nonterminal between-phase condition. Keep original failures, blockers, consumed counts, review debt and PR identity. The coordinator must authenticate a separate named human approval of the exact event/target/current history; a digest alone does not authenticate the person or meaning.
2. Construct only `manifest_sequence_amendment` / `issue123-manifest-sequence-v1` from the exact one-sentence S123-1 replacement. Bind the complete preceding history and strict native target. Check native validation and atomic append. This produces unapproved generation 7 and grants no count, source attempt, key, publication or operation.
3. Apply the already approved held R exact authority only after its conditions are met: `design_reset+1`, all other deltas and seconds zero. Check/reserve reset 4/4, create generation 8, then complete fresh consequential architect design, independent challenge and coordinator approval. Retain remediation debt and require the original owner plus independent final review; initial review cannot substitute.
4. Before discovery, approve the ignored directory, actors, authority references, official-domain queries, host policy, source leads, manifest format, caps, deadline and exclusive attempt accounting. Log discovered URLs and justifications before acquisition. Freeze source-grounded exact listings and remaining attempts before market collection.
5. Before key issuance or publication, establish current direct legal Fund/class and active ISIN evidence, raw bodies and locators where available, full inventory/comparison/continuity dispositions and each current proof constituent. Obtain the distinct human reviewer's actual approval of that proof. Issue bounded keys, then obtain genuine assertions about actual key/envelope bytes and times. Publish only once against the empty parent; reconcile returned envelope, blob and pointer after confirmed success. Stop after failure or uncertainty without retry or market collection requiring that envelope.

Keep the existing acquisition, Yahoo, BND, registry, reference, freshness, financial and legacy limits in ADR 0045. Do not use synthetic tests as actor, source, proof, inventory or publication evidence. This runbook does not execute the transition or authorize Issue117, a trade, or an external financial write.
