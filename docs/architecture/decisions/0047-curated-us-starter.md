The starter is a distinct explicit profile selection. It packages twenty US ETF leads and accepts one locally prepared, immutable, source-attributed artifact. The bounded Yahoo collection checks the exact returned symbol, an admitted US exchange, ETF classification, US market, USD quote currency and original quote observation time. A singleton duplicate group asserts only one exact provider/exchange/symbol listing. Equal names, indexes or families do not establish legal Fund equality or independent investment risk. No ISIN, active-class inventory or curator registry proof is asserted.

One artifact includes all twenty ordered outcomes, a schema-2 research snapshot for collected candidates, the original source times, and a digest. The selected profile binds the universe and artifact digests. The existing retrieval operation consumes this artifact without Chroma or Neo4j; evidence embeds it for offline review, replay and revision checks. All collected candidates enter existing screening. A deterministic ten-candidate shortlist precedes unchanged portfolio subset construction and cent arithmetic. Excluded or unattempted candidates retain reasons. Legacy, US/EU and curator markers and saved serialization remain separate.

The ignored local artifact is development evidence, not a published data feed. Source success and source freshness are distinct: preparing or restoring an artifact never renews observation time. The source can become stale, and a missing concentration or unsupported category still excludes a candidate. No trade, external financial write, legal identity certification or old-review migration is authorized. Issue 141 supersedes only the desired starter outcome of Issue 123; it does not alter Issue 123's ledger, authority, blockers or protected checkout.
